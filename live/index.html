Step 1 of 3

1. Who you serve, or your ideal client.

You already know more of this than you think.

From memory, name 3 to 5 people you’re glad to work with. Tick only the marks that are true for each person. Leave the rest blank. You do not need to dig through old records first.

Step 1 of 3 · One sitting, from memory. Aim for about 10 minutes for all three.

Write first names only. A few is enough. The people with the most ticks are the ones to build your sentence around.

Stuck here? Can’t think of 3 favorites? Start with the last customer you were glad to see.

Example

Maya

  • Again
  • Paid
  • Fair
  • Smooth
  • Referred

Again = I’d gladly do this again. Paid = paid on time. Fair = the pay felt fair. Smooth = the work went smoothly. Referred = they sent someone or left a review.

Stuck here? Think about what they all asked for, or thanked you for.

Step 3 of 3

3. Your business in one sentence.

Stuck? The help is at the bottom of this section.

Don’t list your services. Write what is better for the customer when you’re done. Use the words your favorites already say. A friend should know what you do after hearing it once.
